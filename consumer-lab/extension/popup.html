ORBIT / LOCAL CONNECTOR

Your history.
Your permission.

With your approval, this extension reads visited URLs, page titles, and visit times for the period you select in the dashboard (7, 30, or 90 days).

Chrome’s history permission technically covers your full browsing history and permits modification. Orbit only reads the selected period and never modifies history. It does not read passwords, cookies, tokens, or private account contents.

Data transfers only to connornee-1.github.io/connors-repository/consumer-lab/ in your browser. There are no external uploads or persistent records. Titles can contain personal information. Shared devices may mix people’s activity.
